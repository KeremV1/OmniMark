### ocrs models

OCR with the built-in engine uses the pre-trained [ocrs](https://github.com/robertknight/ocrs) text
detection and recognition models (about 12 MB). They are **not** part of the installer: OmniMark downloads
them only when you choose to, and verifies their checksums. The models are published under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
(<https://huggingface.co/robertknight/ocrs>) and were trained on
[HierText](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/hiertext) (CC BY-SA 4.0) and synthetic data. The
`ocrs` and `rten` engine crates are dual-licensed Apache-2.0 OR MIT and appear in the Rust section.
The models are used as unmodified data files and are not redistributed with OmniMark.
